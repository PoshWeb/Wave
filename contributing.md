# Contributing

We welcome contributions to Wave!

We may not know as much as you about audio, and your insight may be welcome.

We might be missing functionality that you want,
and are happy to hear about it and even happier if you help implement it.

Please consider contributing by:

* [Starring this Repository](https://github.com/PoshWeb/Wave)
* [Opening an Issue](https://github.com/PoshWeb/Wave/issues)
* [Filing a Pull Request](https://github.com/PoshWeb/Wave/pulls)
* [Sponsoring Us](https://github.com/StartAutomating)
