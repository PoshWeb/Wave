# Who Makes Wave

We do!

Wave was initially implemented by [James Brundage (`@StartAutomating`)](https://github.com/StartAutomating).

Wave was heavily helped by a number of people:

* [Jeffrey Snover](https://www.jsnover.com/) came up with PowerShell, and helped us understand musical languages
* [Bruce Payette](https://github.com/BrucePay) wrote PowerShell, and has been helping to refine Wave.
* [Steven Bailey](https://github.com/SrBlackVoid) is a PowerShell enthusaist with a masters in music, and has been helping to codify music theory in PowerShell
* [Steven Judd](https://github.com/StevenJudd) is a PowerShell community lumniary, and has been helping us understand more music theory.

## Who Can Make Waves?

We can!

We all interact with waves every second of every day.

Lightwaves hit our eyes, and we see.

Soundwaves hit our ears, and we hear.

Wave gives us all a free and open source toolkit for making and manipulating waves.

We can all make music.  We can sample, synthesize, and sequence songs.
